We are the Artificial Intelligence Research Centre in the School of Science & Technology at [City St George's, University of London](https://www.citystgeorges.ac.uk/).

Our work bridges the gap between fundamental artificial intelligence research and real-world impact. We collaborate across disciplines to design intelligent systems that are interpretable, efficient, and responsible. 

Through teaching, innovation, and open research, we aim to shape the future of AI in science, healthcare, and society.

### Our Vision
At **AI Centre@CSG**, we aim to advance the frontiers of artificial intelligence through interdisciplinary collaboration between computer science and other diverse domains. Our vision is to build ethical, transparent, and human-centred AI solutions that empower society and improve wellbeing.

### Our Research
Our research spans a diverse range of fields including machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and biomedical AI. We work closely with practitioners, scientists, and industry partners to turn cutting-edge research into impactful real-world applications. We collaborate with internal partners, internal e.g. the [School of Health and Medical Sciences](https://www.citystgeorges.ac.uk/about/schools/health-sciences), the [Department of Mathematics](https://www.citystgeorges.ac.uk/about/schools/science-technology/mathematics), the [Institute for Creativity and AI](https://www.citystgeorges.ac.uk/research/centres/the-institute-for-creativity-and-ai), and external, e.g. the [Fleming Initiative at Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/Stories/IGHI-Annual-Report-2023-fleming/) and [GSK](https://www.gsk.com/en-gb/media/press-releases/gsk-and-fleming-initiative-scientists-unite-to-target-amr-with-advanced-ai/), [Fujitsu Research](https://global.fujitsu/en-global/technology/research), and the [Alan Turing Institute](https://www.turing.ac.uk/).
